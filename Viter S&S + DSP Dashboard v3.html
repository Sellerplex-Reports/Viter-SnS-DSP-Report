How the numbers are built
The math
  • Churn = cancelled ÷ subscribers at start of month. Cancelled = AMC new sign-ups − change in subscriber balance.
  • Lifetime = 1 ÷ monthly churn (capped at 24 months).
  • CLTV (revenue) = S&S revenue per subscriber per month × lifetime. CLTV (profit) = CLTV revenue × margin.
  • Full-customer CLTV = S&S CLTV × (1 + one-time purchase uplift from AMC).
  • Max cost per new subscriber = CLTV profit × payback share. The bar each channel has to clear.
  • Spend per sub reached = channel spend ÷ new subscribers who saw that channel in the 30 days before signing up.
What would change the picture
  • Margin assumption. 30% is a working estimate. Enter your own margin in the field on the Overview tab.
  • Value beyond S&S. In their first 6 months, new subscribers spend 29% more on one-time purchases on top of S&S (AMC). Full-customer CLTV applies that uplift to the whole lifetime, which assumes the mix holds after month 6.
  • Attribution. "Reached" is exposure, not proof the ad caused the sign-up.
  • Brand mix. Viter Energy churns faster than VALI (≈8.5% vs ≈5.7% a month).
  • Stock. Stockouts in Feb–Mar 2026 drove the highest churn months.
  • Coupons. S&S coupons lift sign-ups on the products that run them (Viter Energy peaked at 16% of S&S sales in Aug 2026). That lift should not be credited to DSP.
Data notes
  • AMC keeps 13 months, so churn and ad-path figures cover Oct 2025 – Sep 2026; trend charts go back to Apr 2025.
  • The ad-path query counts ≈4,250 sign-ups vs ≈4,940 in the new-subscription query; AMC hides small groups. Shares are reliable; the monthly new-subscriber count uses the larger query.
  • "DSP only" and "No ad exposure" were too small for AMC to show by month, so they're yearly totals.
  • Dec 2025 S&S file covers one day; re-pull the full month.
  • Per-product churn uses AMC new subscriptions by ASIN. Products with under ~50 subscribers are flagged as low volume; their figures swing a lot.
